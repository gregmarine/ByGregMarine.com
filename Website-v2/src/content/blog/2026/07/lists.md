I had a seemingly random thought the other day about lists. The "seemingly" part is that I often see or read things that trigger thoughts that eventually come together here. But how random are the things I see or read these days? LOL Some of these things are on social media; which is powered by an algorithm...

One of the posts I came across was this idea of a reverse bucket list. We all know what a bucket list is, right? The reverse of that is to list all of the things we've actually already done instead of all the things we hope to do someday. It is sort of like a gratitude list, I suppose. But more precisely, it's a bucket full of my life's adventures. I can say that I have a lot more in the reverse bucket list than what I've ever tried to put in the typical bucket list. I think I'd like to work on making such a list soon. Perhaps I'll put that on my bucket list...LOL j/k

Another list that popped up on my social media feed recently was about life as a Christian. I don't know if we all do this, but sometimes I get to thinking about all of the things I've done "for the Lord." Not necessarily the kind of list one puts on a resume as if it were a set of accomplishments. Just a general sense of the good I've done in my life. But the post I saw challenges me to toss out that list and instead keep a running list of all the things God has done for me. In a way, I've already started this transition between those lists. These past few years in Bible devotionals and study has been opening my heart to being able to truly witness and experience God's goodness. Although the studies have reminded me off what I've done in the church most of my life, the spotlight has shifted to how God has been using me. I've not been one to say, "hey look at all of the things I've done for the Lord!" Yet, my sinful nature would still keep that list in my heart sometimes as if it were all things I had done on my own. I tossed that out a few years ago, though. And that post reminded me that my focus should have always been on the list of things God has been doing in and through me all this time.

Another list I have on my mind wasn't prompted by a social media post. Instead, it was inspired by the other two lists. I think I'd like to start a list of things I want to remove from my life. All of the things I feel just aren't meant for me or are getting in the way of living the life God is showing me. When I've fasted before, I would typically choose something that was getting in the way of my relationship with God in some way. I remember that candy was a huge problem for me. The year I chose that as a 21 day fast was the last time I was addicted to the stuff. That's a reminder that there are still so many other things in my life that I could remove and be the better for it. That seems like a list worth writing down as well. And then work through the list to improve the life experiences I have.

In some small way, I've started keeping these lists in my heart lately, already. I just think it might be a good thing to actually write down. One of the things keeping me from actually doing this is that I don't want it to become just a habit. I want it to be a passion. I don't want the list to just be a checklist that I mark off. I'd rather it all be an anchor of sorts as I continue to navigate my life. A list of experiences I've had in my life, what God has done in my life, and what I should probably remove from my life seems like a good thing.

Aside from lists, something else that popped up the other day...personality assessments...LOL Specifically, Myers-Briggs. There was a webinar at work on it. It reminded me of the time Cherie asked me about it. She had guessed that I was INFP. At the time, I had no idea what any of that meant or was. She asked some questions and sent me an infographic. It seemed to describe me perfectly. From the webinar, the same assessment was validated.

I've not actually taken the full tests of any of the personality assessments out there. I've actively avoided it, actually. In my life, people have weaponized things that like against me. The same with the Bible. Some of the abuse I've experienced came from people using such things against me in some way. A means to get what they wanted from me. It all kind of triggers my fight or flight sometimes. But when Cherie asked me about it a few years ago, I wasn't triggered at all. By the time this came up in conversation with her, we had a well established relationship of trust. Everything about her showed me that she was more interested in understanding me than to use me. It felt safe and I'm glad she brought it up. She and I both learned something about me that day...hehehe

Note and Paintsprout are coming along nicely. Biblesprout has stalled...LOL With Notesprout, it's my daily driver for notes. It has been for quite a while, actually. I'm not fully using it for writing yet, though. I'm working on some features at the moment to help with that. Once those features are polished, I'll be back to drafting blog posts by hand and then converting for publishing. Plus I have other writing that I strongly desire to get going...hehehe

For Paintsprout, I took a step back after the proof-of-concept stage and I'm now working my way towards working on some real art that has been rolling around in my brain. I did a sketch the other day of Jack from "The Nightmare before Christmas." That's officially the first sketch from Paintsprout. But the original file is lost forever...LOL I did that on a test build of the app and those files got blown away. I accidentally did that. But now I'm at the stage where I have both a development and stable build. So, I'll now do official work from the stable build and will backup those files.

Biblesprout is something I use most days still. But I'm sort of in a holding pattern with it as I decide how I want to move forward with it. Some of its features cross paths with Notesprout. So, do I copy Notesprout features into Biblesprout? Do I move Biblesprout into a feature of Notesprout? Or do I get them to interoperate with each other? Something else to consider with Biblesprout is that it has cross platform uses. How I use it on e-ink is different than what I'd use it for on my phone or on a website. Whereas Notesprout is pretty much only useful on e-ink.

All of the sprouts have their various strengths on each platform, actually. Paintsprout is strongest on a full powered art tablet. Although it could have sketch features on e-ink. Biblesprout would be a great reference tool on my phone and could be integrated for Bible text on my website. Notesprout is great for draft on e-ink while also being able to export to my website. Normally, I'd be overwhelmed with all of these options. However, since I'm using Claude Code to build it all, I can dream that big without getting lost in it all.
