The last few months have been interesting. I've been wanting to write about our May vacation ever since I got back. I just haven't been in the right headspace to write about it. That isn't a bad thing. My headspace has been good for the most part. It just hasn't been in a "write about an amazing vacation" state...LOL I've learned that when I want something to be done right, I need to be in that zone, you know?

So, I'm finally where I can write about our first day. I had already created the vlog weeks ago...LOL Now, it's time to write about the day and present the vlog as well. The day before was my travel day. It was okay. The route I've been taking this year hasn't been the greatest. So, I'm going to reevaluate flights for future trips. It's always good to try new flights and see how they go. It was worth a shot. I just didn't like it...LOL But arrive in plenty of time to get our rental, checkin at the hotel, and have a lovely dinner at the attached restaurant that they have at ALO. The other Ayres hotel doesn't have a restaurant. This felt like a special treat! And it was pretty good. I had authentic tacos that had handmade tortillas they made fresh when I ordered. That was a first for me!

For this trip, I decided to try and keep everything I packed into a single backpack. I now have one of those that you can vacuum seal the clothes and reduce the space needed. It worked rather well. The caveat was that it prevented me from bringing home my traditional coffee mug. Lesson learned! My motivation was that Southwest Airlines is inappropriately aggressive about telling people that there is limited overhead bin storage. They are constantly reminding people that they will need to check roller bags if the bin gets full. That stresses me out every time I have my roller bag with me. The funny thing is that they always have room when I board the plane. And one of my last flights had extra room to spare this time. That was despite the guy coming over the speaker telling people that we were going to run late because people had too many things in the overhead bin. Yeah, it was very wrong. And I wonder if I should start reporting a complaint to Southwest about this...LOL Travel is stressful enough without that nonsense.

I woke pretty early on our first day of vacation. It's probably because of my timezone and how I'm not longer really on my offset sleep schedule anymore. But I did manage to fall back to sleep and got a little more shuteye...hehehe Shortly after I woke for the day, I made my way down for breakfast. It is buffet style on ALO. That meant it wasn't as fresh. But I still enjoyed it. There is an outdoor patio at ALO and I naturally decided to eat out there. That morning, it was better to be outside anyway. There was a school group that morning pretty much taking up the entire indoor seating area...LOL I don't mind the number of people. It just meant there weren't any tables available for just me. I would have had to sit at the bar if I wanted to eat inside. So, I'm glad they had the patio for that reason as well. But I typically prefer to eat outside anyway...hehehe

They didn't have apple juice available for free. But they did have it in their little "lobby shop." So, I bought some for our vacation. We were able to have it both days we had breakfast together. Tradition saved! LOL After that, I went back to my room. I wrote the letters for Cherie and Bambi. I like to write those sort of letters in the moment. I have those special feelings on these vacations. The things I write in the moment are the best for these things. I sometimes write stuff ahead of time. But I like the feeling of writing in the hotel...hehehe I then steamed all of my clothes to prepare them for the adventure that awaited!
