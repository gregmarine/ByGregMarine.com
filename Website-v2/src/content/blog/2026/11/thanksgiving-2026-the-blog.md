Although I recorded a vlog from my day at EPCOT yesterday for Thanksgiving, I don't plan to edit and publish it until after I'm finished editing and publishing my vlog from the November 2026 vacation with Cherie last weekend. However, I wanted to still write a little about the day, today. So, this is Thanksgiving 2026: The Blog!

I wasn't really sure if I'd go to Disney World for Thanksgiving or just stay home. I was invited to my sister's family gathering and there was at one moment a temptation to fly up to Indiana for Thanksgiving up there. I did decide not to do any family things again this year, though. But it wasn't until I got back from last weekend's vacation before I finally decided to actually go to Disney World.

I almost decided to start the day at Animal Kingdom and then hop over to EPCOT to finish the day. And briefly, I thought about doing a 4 park day. But I realized I just wanted to stick to one park. EPCOT was the logical choice. As I was planning my day, I also thought it would be nice to enjoy a few restaurants this time since the last festival was over and the next wasn't going to start until today. Between festivals is a great time to enjoy their table service places.

Before I go any further into the day, I did want to briefly touch on why I once again didn't do a family event. I feel as though I've mentioned before that a few things happened around the holidays that take up a bit of my mental energy. Those events still get to me a bit. Albeit, not like they used to. They no longer have any power over me. I just don't feel ready to jump back into family time at Thanksgiving or Christmas. I tried the big family Christmas a few years ago. I didn't do well, in reality. I still go up to be with my parents and brother's family for Christmas. But Thanksgiving is still a no-go for me. The worst of things happened on Thanksgiving...LOL

I do not use those moments as an excuse to treat people poorly. Especially this time of year. Instead, I do what I need to do in order to mentally handle life and still share my joy and love for life with others. That sometimes means I have to say no to things I know will trigger the darkness that dims my internal light. If I don't set necessary boundaries, I would lose myself again. Nobody wants that. I think I'm finding healthy ways of dealing with it. Healing is happening and I know that someday Thanksgiving will be a part of my life's celebrations again. I've already found ways to celebrate Christmas again. And in my own way, I did celebrate Thanksgiving yesterday...hehehe

I dressed all dapper, of course. The weather was perfect for the attire...hehehe It wasn't too cool and wasn't too hot. It was the Goldilocks of weather days...LOL I went with a Christmas green, red, and white theme. I got a lot of compliments. And even one family thanked me for bringing my vibe to the parks...hehehe It reminded me of what my brother told me on my birthday this year when I was with him and dressed in my dapper Figment bound. Their comments were almost verbatim what Andrew said to me that day!

I of course brought Cherie and Petey along for the adventure. But I also brought Klyde with me! For Cherie and Petey, it was their photos on a pair of e-ink frames. But Klyde, a little bunny that Cherie's mom made, was there "in person." LOL Petey was in all of the food photos and Kylde had his own photos and videos to tell his story throughout the day.

I loved having the photo printer Cherie gave me. I had done 2 test prints at home to try it out. But I saved 3 of the included sheets for my day at EPCOT. I chose 3 very specific places to take the photos and print them immediately. I went with France because that's where I had breakfast. And I chose Japan because that's where I had lunch/dinner. For the third, I decided to do the main Christmas tree. I snapped each photo and immediately printed them. I then took a photo of me holding each print near where I took the original photo just to show where each was taken and printed...hehehe I LOVED IT and plan to make this a regular thing!
