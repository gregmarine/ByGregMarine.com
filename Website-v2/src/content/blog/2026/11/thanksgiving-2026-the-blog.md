Although I recorded a vlog from my day at EPCOT on Thanksgiving, I don't plan to edit and publish it until after I'm finished editing and publishing my vlog from the November 2026 vacation with Cherie. However, I wanted to still write a little about the day, today. So, this is Thanksgiving 2026: The Blog!

I wasn't really sure if I'd go to Disney World for Thanksgiving or just stay home. I was invited to my sister's family gathering and there was at one moment a temptation to fly up to Indiana for Thanksgiving up there. I did decide not to do any family things again this year. But it wasn't until I got back from last weekend's vacation before I finally decided to actually go to Disney World.

I almost decided to start the day at Animal Kingdom and then hop over to EPCOT to finish the day. But I realized I just wanted to stick to one park. EPCOT was the logical choice. As I was planning my day, I also thought it would be nice to enjoy a few restaurants this time since the next last festival was over and the next wasn't going to start until today. Between festivals is a great time to enjoy their table service places.
