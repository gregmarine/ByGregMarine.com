Although I recorded a vlog from my day at EPCOT yesterday for Thanksgiving, I don't plan to edit and publish it until after I'm finished editing and publishing my vlog from the November 2026 vacation with Cherie last weekend. However, I wanted to still write a little about the day, today. So, this is Thanksgiving 2026: The Blog!

I wasn't really sure if I'd go to Disney World for Thanksgiving or just stay home. I was invited to my sister's family gathering and there was at one moment a temptation to fly up to Indiana for Thanksgiving up there. I finally decided not to do any family things again this year, though. But it wasn't until I got back from last weekend's vacation before I finally decided to actually go to Disney World.

I almost decided to start the day at Animal Kingdom and then hop over to EPCOT to finish the day. And briefly, I thought about doing a 4 park day. But I realized I just wanted to stick to one park. EPCOT was the logical choice. When I asked Cherie which park I should do, that's the one she suggested. Plus, it is technically my favorite park when people ask me. As I was planning my day, I also thought it would be nice to enjoy a few restaurants this time since the last festival was over and the next wasn't going to start until today. Between festivals is a great time to enjoy their table service places.

I dressed all dapper, of course. The weather was perfect for the attire...hehehe It wasn't too cool and wasn't too hot. It was the Goldilocks of weather days...LOL I went with a Christmas green, red, and white theme. I got a lot of compliments. And even one family thanked me for bringing my vibe to the parks...hehehe It reminded me of what my brother told me on my birthday this year when I was with him and dressed in my dapper Figment bound. Their comments were almost verbatim what Andrew said to me that day!

I of course brought Cherie and Petey along for the adventure. But I also brought Klyde with me! For Cherie and Petey, it was their photos on a pair of e-ink frames. But Klyde, a little bunny that Cherie's mom made, was there "in person." LOL Petey was in all of the food photos and Kylde had his own photos and videos to tell his story throughout the day. Cherie was there when I had my meals and for the park arrival selfie...hehehe

I loved having the photo printer Cherie gave me. I had done 2 test prints at home to try it out. But I saved 3 of the included sheets for my day at EPCOT. I chose 3 very specific places to take the photos and print them immediately. I went with France because that's where I had breakfast. And I chose Japan because that's where I had lunch/dinner. For the third, I decided to do the main Christmas tree after it was dark out. I snapped each photo and immediately printed them. I then took a photo of me holding each print near where I took the original photo just to show where each was taken and printed...hehehe I LOVED IT and plan to make this a regular thing!

I didn't mention this in my vlog, but when I was in line for the Christmas tree PhotoPass, the family in front of me was a little...restless. Personally, I felt the children were quite well behaved. It was the parents who seemed to be agitated at the kids...LOL It was a bit weird, actually. The parents and grandparents were yelling at the 3 little boys as if they had just done the worst thing imaginable. Yet, those boys weren't doing anything really. I felt for the kids for sure. But it was funny how when they were getting their pictures taken, the entire family was standing all perfect and super happy. It was as if nothing had just happened...LOL I kind of wish I could have shown them how they all looked in contrast and how actually well behaved the boys were being. I just wonder if something had happened before I saw them...LOL

I actually saw a number of unhappy families yesterday. For a day of low crowds, I was surprised to see so many folks upset at each other. I've seen that happen on hot and busy days. But it was cool and not crowded at all...LOL I even overheard a few young ladies talking about trauma as they walked near me around the World Showcase. They were probably young enough to my kids and they were pushing strollers with whom I would assume were their kids.

Generational trauma is a real thing. But the biggest problem is that people keep doing it because they feel entitled. They seem to think that if they were traumatized then it is okay for them to act out themselves and pass that along. Of course, I'm no expert on the matter and can only speak from my own experiences. I never want what I went through in my life to be an excuse for me to treat others in a bad way. I'd rather stop the madness and instead share joy and love. Nothing that has ever happened to me needs to impact others in an equally or more negative way than what I went through, you know?

My only real complaint about yesterday, though, was that some of the attractions I went on had people who really weren't there to enjoy the attraction. They were talking the whole time. It took a little away from my experience with the attraction. And I imagine it impacts others as well. Especially folks who may have never been there before. I've dealt with that most of my life in various experiences where I tried to be in the moment and others around me weren't there for the experience.

In a way, I feel for those people. I can't imagine bringing all of life's noise to a place that should be full of happiness and magic. We all have "life" things going on. But I also feel that we all need to "get away" sometimes. And the people I'm thinking about right now were in such a place to get away from life. So, it's not like they don't have the opportunity to "escape." They are just choosing to bring life's burdens with them. It's their choice. But it seems like a bad choice...LOL

Despite all of that, it was one of my best experiences at Disney World. The few things that would otherwise take away my joy and happiness didn't really take all of that away from me anyway. I had a fantastic time and my heart was so full. It has been full for a week now. This past week has been so amazing. Between my vacation with Cherie and how well my day at EPCOT was yesterday, I feel as though the past 7 days have been perfect. Probably the best week of my entire life, actually.

All that I've been experiencing is a gift from God. I'm grateful that He allows me to experience and witness His goodness. Especially what He does through me for others. Life has its highs and lows, ups and downs. This past week feels very much like a balancing of some of the downs and lows I've had lately. It feels good to have highs and ups now.

I may have more to say after I've edited the vlog from the day. If I do, I'll include that in the post I'll make for the vlog. That may take another week or so. It is taking quite a while to transfer the videos to my Apple account...LOL So, I wanted to try to do something from my Google account since that's the primary source now.

I ordered some new photo sheets for my photo printer! I got a set of the kind like the ones that came with the printer. And I also got a set that has a double sticker. I'm so excited!!!
