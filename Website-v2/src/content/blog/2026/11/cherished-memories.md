Well, our November 2026 vacation is now a memory! And a cherished one at that! Wow! What an amazing experience!!! I'll have a lot more to say in the coming days as I process it all, edit the vlog, and write about it here in my blog.

I was originally going to just let the vlog speak for itself. But after reading Cherie's latest entry (as of writing this), I'm inspired to still write some things in blog form. There is something different about writing and video. So, it will be good to capture it all both ways...hehehe

Prior to our vacation, I had started a blog entry that I was writing in for a few days. It never made it to published...LOL After reading it again this morning, I've decided it didn't need to be published after all. I was a little stuck for words but still wanted to write...LOL Well, I'm unstuck now...LOL This vacation was the renewal my spirit needed.

My heart is so full right now. I can't even explain how good I feel at the moment. It isn't one of those "vacation highs" or anything like that. It's more of a sense of contentment that comes from enjoying everything for what it is. Especially with the heart posture of knowing that every moment is a gift from God.

I'll likely write more about this when the time comes, but I had an amazing "God moment" yesterday on my drive down from Orange to San Diego. I was seeing the Pacific Ocean off to my right and thought I should probably plan to stop at the ocean on my next visit. At about the moment I had that thought, I saw a sign for what they call a "Vista View" on the side of the freeway. I thought about checking out the map sometime so I would be sure to stop at one on my next trip. But as I approached it and saw what it was, I decided to stop this time!

I love the Pacific Ocean. It's the largest body of water on the planet. The first time I saw it, I couldn't see much because it was a foggy day...LOL Maybe a few hundred yards of water is all I could see...LOL That was July 5th, 2024. Well, yesterday, I could see the full 12 miles or so that one can see based on the curvature of the earth. The sky had very little cloud coverage and the sun was shining brightly on the deep blue water. I was in awe of the majesty of God's creation. Creation was declaring His glory in that moment between the ocean and mountains nearby. I was glad to have my new cameras because I was able to capture so amazing views!

The west coast seagulls I experienced in that moment were far more mellow than the west coast seagulls I have here in Florida...LOL They were just hanging out with me and appeared in some of my photos and videos. They didn't make a sound, though. They were so chill in comparison to the seagulls here on the west coast of Florida...hehehe

I always enjoy my time with Cherie on our vacations. But there was something extra special about this experience. Earlier this year, I figured out I feel at home when I'm with her. This time felt even deeper than that. I even told her that I feel as though she's a calming spirit even while I'm driving in LA traffic. She laughed at that because she told me she's not really known as a calming spirit...hehehe I wonder if it is just that she is so calm being with me too? There's a special kind of God-gifted peace when we are together.

This vacation is full of cherished memories. I'm so glad I was able to vlog about it all throughout the experience. As always, I didn't record every single thing. I did just enough to take us back to the many moments we experienced. At the Disney parks, I did capture something from each thing we did instead of writing down a list of everything...hehehe So, we at least know what we did and in what order despite not taking my notes this time. All that to say, it was so easy to remain in the moment throughout these cherished memories.

I don't think it is fair to all of the moments to pick a favorite...hehehe I could list a few things that didn't work out well on the vacation. Such as, the apple juice machine was broken and 3 of the 4 breakfasts had nasty coffee...LOL But there was one moment that stands out a little. The snowp at Downtown Disney on Saturday was a special moment. I captured some of it; which includes some of Cherie's reaction to it. Yet, the initial reaction is a memory I have just in my mind. I wasn't recording when it first fell from the sky. The look at Cherie's face is priceless and I'm so glad my eyes saw it. See the video that I did take will always remind me of what I saw just before...hehehe
