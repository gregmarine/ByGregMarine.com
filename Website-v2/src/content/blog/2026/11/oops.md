At the beginning of 2023, I began a health journey that brought me to a place I never thought I'd ever be at again. I had a breakthrough in my health I wasn't expecting. And I'm still reaping many of the benefits of that journey today. But at the beginning of 2025, I started to go on what I called "maintenance mode." I also did something I didn't really plan on doing...LOL I stopped exercising! What?!? Ever since, I've been very inconsistent at my walking. And I completely stopped jogging. In January 2025, I still walked regularly. But the winter weather that year put a damper on my routine and I never got back to it.

I still feel pretty good overall today. And I'm still in a sort of "maintenance mode" with my diet. I eat mostly healthy with those "cheat" meals and snacks thrown in a bit. But I had noticed my appearance was changing since last year. I haven't really noticed a decline in energy or anything like that. Yet, I still feel a little off on a few things. Last week, I weighed myself for the first time in ages. I was shocked at the results. Since the beginning of 2025, I have gained 35 lbs!!! What?!? I was so shocked that I checked again. And then the next day, I checked again...LOL Yep...I'm up 35 lbs...Oops!

It would seem I need to go on another health journey...hehehe Albeit, not quite the extreme health makeover I went through nearly 4 years ago...LOL Like I said, my diet is still going well. So, very little adjustment, if any, is needed there. And as I also said, my energy level is still quite good. I mostly need to get back into an exercise routine. I'm thinking that going back to regular walking routine is likely the best thing for me right now. I don't need to lose 35 lbs, though. 25 at most. I'll make it a flexible goal to lose 25 by the end of 2027...hehehe

I watched the first 3 episodes of season 6 of "The Chosen" last night. I hadn't really planned to do that yet. But the opportunity presented itself and I took advantage of the time last night. They are the only episodes available so far. I'm not sure when the next 3 will drop. And the "exciting" conclusion won't be until Spring next year. I don't like that they are making the season finale only available in theaters when it's released. I have no clue when it will be available to watch at home. I don't currently plan to watch it in theaters...because it's a TV show. I haven't watched any of the previous seasons on their theatrical releases either...again...because it's a TV show...LOL I have opinions, don't I?! LOL

I really enjoyed these first 3 episodes. I love how they are telling the story and I'm happy with the artistic license they are taking. The other day I mentioned the controversy about the way they represented the angels. Well, I found it to be really good, myself. The teaser that got some folks all upset definitely wasn't enough to form an opinion, in my opinion...hehehe Having now seen the scenes where they appear, my opinion is a positive one. I may have even clapped a little...hehehe

There is a lot of subjective "fill in the gaps" dialog in these episodes. Especially surrounding the disciples' reaction to Jesus' arrest and the initial questioning of Jesus. But I really loved how they did it. I especially love how they are doing the Judas character. These characters are becoming very relatable...to me at least.

Again, "The Chosen" isn't Gospel. But like any good Christian content should do, it points me to Jesus. It is doing a good job at reminding me what faith is. And no matter what circumstances I am facing, it is a great reminder to look to Jesus' teachings instead of giving into my anxieties and fears. The Jesus character specifically instructed the disciples to do just that...

I got an email from UPS telling me about a delivery scheduled for me from Amazon. Since I have some outstanding orders with Amazon, I assumed it was one of those orders. The final notification was that it had been delivered today. But the photo was clearly not my door...LOL I didn't even recognize the festive decor on the door in the photo. I looked around at my neighbors on mine and the second floor of my building and none of them had anything like it. I didn't go up to the third floor. I called the apartment complex's office to see if anyone reported it to them. Nope, nothing. Since it was raining, I got out my umbrella and checked the first floor of the building next to mine. Nope...not there either. So, I decided to report this to Amazon. As it turns out, none of my orders were being shipped via UPS...Oops! I have no clue why UPS notified me of a package delivery. Perhaps someone in my building also got something from Amazon today and UPS just sent me the notification because the building number matches my address? OOOrrr...someone sent me a gift and it was still delivered to the wrong address...LOL I've received surprise gifts from people before that were sent from Amazon...hehehe
