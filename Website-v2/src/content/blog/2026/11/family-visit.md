Happy November!!! Sure, it's already 10 days into the month...LOL But since this is my first blog entry this month, why not celebrate the new month now?!

Life has been life'in lately. And I haven't been writing at all about it...LOL You know how I have said that writing helps me process my thoughts? Yeah, I haven't been processing things like I would prefer lately. That's just how life has been. I'm doing well overall. I'm just in a season that I don't like while at the same time being grateful for the growth it brings.

In the past, I would describe such a time as a spinning top that is starting to wobble a bit. While things aren't quite as balanced as I would like them to be, I wouldn't describe my current state quite like a wobbling spinning top. I'd describe it more like "The Secret Life of Walter Mitty." Things out of my control are happening all around me while I continue to do what I'm supposed to do and going along on the adventures that comes with it. I have my moments of daydreaming. And then I step back into reality long enough to find out I raced into the path of an erupting volcano. After being saved from the volcano, I then find myself on the side of a mountain looking through a lens at a snow leopard without snapping a photograph just so I can live in the moment. Although one won't find a photo of me on the cover Life magazine at the end of this season, I oddly find that movie relatable to what "now" feels like.

Anywho...LOL My parents came down for a visit last week. I'm grateful for their visit. We all seemed to have a good time. It could have just been me, but I also felt this weird vibe the entire time. I felt slightly out of place. But it's probably just in my own head. Or at least, I highly doubt anyone else would admit that there was something "in the air" last week...LOL My family isn't known for talking about things...

I worked 2 of the days last week. My sister and her husband had to work some as well. So, Mom and Dad also spent some time visiting with Dad's Aunt and Uncle that live on the other side of the state. Sounds like they had a good time over there. Also, each night of the days I visited with everyone, I drove back home. They all wanted me to stay at my sister's house. But I really wanted to be home each night. So, that's what I did. It wasn't anything really against any of them. It's was just what I needed for me.

Some of the highlights from the week include mini golf, shopping, lots of food, and a short trip to Sunken Gardens. I think my favorite time was shopping with Mom on Monday. We used to spend a lot of one on one time together when I was young. Being with her that day brought back some warm feelings from years past. She asked if I was okay just shopping and I told her how much I loved it. I don't know many men who love to shop...LOL I don't always buy things. But I absolutely love to browse at least. And spending that time with Mom was the best!

I remember this one time when "Titanic" was coming out on VHS. Mom and I stood in line at a department store so we could purchase it at midnight...LOL It was one of those rare times when we wanted to do something like that. It was just her and I in that line. It was a wonderful mother/son moment. Although I was far from being a dad back then, I cracked one of my favorite dad jokes that night..."Is this the line for the bathroom?!" LOL Everyone around us laughed...HAHAHA

Mom and Dad made it home safely Sunday evening. Before they left to go home, I went out to breakfast with them on Saturday. It has become one of our traditions when they leave to go come. It was a bittersweet moment after a bittersweet visit. I'm grateful for my time with them and my sister's new family. Some new and wonderful memories were made last week. I'm also grateful it is all just a memory now, though.
