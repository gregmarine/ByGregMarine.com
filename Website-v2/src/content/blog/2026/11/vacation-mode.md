We have another real vacation coming up this weekend. And my mind is already settling into vacation mode...LOL When I started writing this entry, I still had another day of work left. And I was productive. But I am also sooooo ready for this vacation. Work has been workin' me. And a few personal things have taken up some brain power too. This vacation couldn't have come at a better time...hehehe

I've been tracking the weather since last week and it remains a good forecast. Especially the temperatures. It should be a great time to be all dappered up. I'll have to wear my thermal undershirt to keep warm, actually; which I absolutely love. One of the challenges with dressing dapper is that warm or hot weather can be difficult with all the layers. I'm typically comfortable. But there have been times when the heat got to me here in Florida. I've never had any issues being dapper in California. This week looks to be the perfect temperature to be all dappered up!

Once again, there will be some firsts. That feels amazing considering how many times we have vacationed these past few years. Oh, and an ornament scavenger hunt at Downtown Disney is a first this year. This is all so exciting!!! OOOH! And the train ride with all the Christmas decor!!! Yay!!!

I'm hoping to use my latest experience with vlogging for this vacation. I'll record some personal thoughts on the hotel breaks. And I'll try to capture some of our experiences throughout the trip. I've learned how to capture snippets that help tell the story of the day while remaining present in the moment. I don't vlog in the way those influencers do. I do a more "this is my day" style and it works well for me. And I sprinkle in some deep thoughts from time to time as well...hehehe
