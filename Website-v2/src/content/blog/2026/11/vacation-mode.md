We have another real vacation coming up in a few days. And my mind is already settling into vacation mode...LOL I still have another day of work left. And I'll be productive. But I am also sooooo ready for this vacation.

I've been tracking the weather since last week and it remains a good forecast. Especially the temperatures. It should be a great time to be all dappered up. I'll have to wear my thermal undershirt to keep warm, actually; which I absolutely love. One of the challenges with dressing dapper is that warm or hot weather can be difficult with all the layers. I'm typically comfortable. But there have been times when the heat got to me. Especially here in Florida. This week looks to be the perfect temperature to be all dappered up!

Once again, there will be some firsts. That feels amazing considering how many times we have vacationed these past few years. This time, we should experience "snoap" at Downtown Disney for the first time. And we may get to experience the Indiana Jones attraction. For me, I'll also experience the Alice in Wonderland attraction for the first time. And then also for me, the rose garden and natural history museum. Oh, and an ornament scavenger hunt at Downtown Disney is a first this year. This is all so exciting!!! OOOH! And the train ride with all the Christmas decor!!! Yay!!!

I'm hoping to use my latest experience with vlogging for this vacation. I'll record some personal thoughts from the hotel breaks. And I'll try to capture some of our experiences throughout the trip. I've learned how to capture snippets that help tell the story of the day without it keeping me from being present in the moment. I don't vlog in the way those influencers do. I do a more "this is my day" style and it works well for me. And I sprinkle in some deep thoughts from time to time as well...hehehe
