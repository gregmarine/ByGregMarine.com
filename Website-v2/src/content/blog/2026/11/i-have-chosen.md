Three blog entries three days in a row!?! Wwwhhhaaattt?!? HAHAHA Yep, that's right, I'm back for more thoughts...hehehe

The main topic on my mind for this entry is the upcoming season 6 of "The Chosen." I believe the first few episodes drop on Prime this weekend. I'm looking forward to seeing what they've done with the story this season. I have...Chosen...the continue watching the show...hehehe

I still remember when the show was first suggested to me. I had just recently moved to Florida, I believe. My renewed dedication to following Jesus had just happened less than a year before. I was talking on the phone with Mom and Dad about finding a new church or something and they mentioned I should watch this "new" show called "The Chosen."

I didn't actually start watching it until a few years later. But having looked into it, I had already formed the opinion that I wanted to approach the show as being entertainment and not straight Gospel representation. I somewhat knew that a TV show was going to need to embellish at least a little because the "source content" didn't go into enough detail to actually build a show...LOL Especially the planned 7 seasons.

After I finally started to watch it, I did come across some articles online from people who were tearing it apart. I found those articles just as entertaining as the show itself. Mind you, I'm using the term "entertainment" loosely here for both the show itself and the commentary. People tend to have a lot of opposing opinions of the show and take every opportunity to "teach" and "reveal" the "truth" about "The Chosen."

Once again, there are already opinions flying about concerning this new season. The teaser showed some angels coming to Jesus' rescue as he is about to be arrested at the garden. I've seen the teaser and I think I saw 3 different representations of these angels. Jesus calls them off with a simple nod. It seems harmless to me. But the "experts" criticized that these angels look like various warriors from places like Africa and Native American cultures. To which I say...yeah, so what?

One of the "critics" sent a message calling for the firing of the creative people behind that decision. The shows creators had an equally silly response, in my opinion. They didn't call out the person by name. But they did make it part of their social media campaigns. The only response from that that I agree with so far is that "certainty can become someone's idol."

It is so easy to believe that we are always right about something. Whether it be our views on the Bible, or how we respond to opposing opinion, if we stand firm in pride for what we "know" to be "true", we are making "our truth" an idol of sorts.

I don't really like the negative opinions of how the angels are represented. We don't really know what actual angels look like. So, the decision to make them look like warriors from various real cultures doesn't seem like a bad thing to me. After all, we don't know what Jesus actually looked like, do we? I highly doubt he was anything like a caucasian dude from the United States...LOL

I equally don't like how the folks from "The Chosen" have made public what I believe was a private email sent to them. And then turning that private note into a social media spectacle that has further brought out the ugly of today's Christianity. People are praising "The Chosen" for the way they are basically bullying the person who wrote that message.

Opinions are like butts...we all have them and most of them stink...LOL All of my thoughts on this is just opinion as well. I'll not idolize my thoughts and I'm definitely not certain of any of it. I've lived quite a bit of my life certain I knew the truth about all sorts of things. I'm not so certain about much of those things anymore. One thing I'm very certain of is that I'm not on a journey in search of God's Truth and no longer looking for mine.
