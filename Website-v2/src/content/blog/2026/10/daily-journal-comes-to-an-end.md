It kind of goes without saying, but I haven't been blogging much lately. I keep wanting to get back into a rhythm...a fascinating rhythm...LOL But I haven't really found it. Same is true with social media. I seem to have lost my way with some things these past few years. Especially this year. Yet, in general, I'm not lost at all...hehehe It's just that I miss certain things, like frequently blogging.

I started working on my vlog from the Disney vacation I took a few weeks ago. I discovered that no all of the videos have properly transferred. So, there is a delay in my ability to get that one going. It was 5 total "days" of footage. It was well over 600 individual videos in all...LOL I don't know if I'll include all of them. But I at least want to go through them all before deciding if something is cut. For a vacation like that, I need to come up with a better system for getting the footage organized. So, I'll be building an app or two for that...LOL

Speaking of building apps, I've paused development of the Sproutscape apps. I keep using them daily. But I haven't touched new work on that project in a few weeks. First, there was the Disney vacation. I didn't even touch a computer for days...LOL But then last week, I was resting in the evenings. After work, I just did my Bible study and vegg'ed a bit. And over this past weekend, I just didn't chores and tried to start the vlog. It was good to take a break from software engineering. I had been doing it almost constantly since the spring! It's good to step back for a bit and relax my mind.

That said, I'm not sure my mind is actually relaxing...LOL It seems to always be active. I've even noticed an uptick in how much I dream. I rarely remember the details. But I do remember that I dreamed quite a bit each night. And throughout the day, my mind seems to be going full speed ahead constantly. It probably doesn't help that I'm now using AI augmented development for software because I'm able to offload certain tasks to the AI. That frees my mind to process other things. I've heard people worry about cognitive decline while using AI for software development. But for me, I have experienced the opposite, it would seem. Not that I really know what I'm talking about. But other areas of my mind have suddenly woken up this year...LOL

I'm glad to be slowing down a bit in this moment to write, though. I need moments like this. For example, I've been sketching a lot more lately. Especially on the weekends. That helps in a few areas. First of all, sketching and painting help with my tremor. The controlled muscle movements are therapeutic for that part of my brain. Second of all, it exercises my creativity. Some of the sketches are based on a tutorial. But some of them are coming from my own creative side. Third of all, it has been fun to share the sketches. None of them are what I'd call amazing. But they were all fun and bring a type of joy that only they can. Especially that cute ghost I drew yesterday. It juvenile in nature. But that's the point. It takes me back to my childhood. And I hope it does for others who have seen it.
