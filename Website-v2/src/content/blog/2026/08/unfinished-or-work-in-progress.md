title: Unfinished or Work In Progress
date: 2026-08-30T12:30:00-04:00
tags: []
---
I feel as though I have a lot of unfinished business in my life. But, I also wonder how much of it is just a work in progress? There are things like artwork and writings that are sitting there waiting to be finished or abandoned. All of it was important in the moment it was started. Did it suddenly become unimportant or less important? No, I don't think so. It feels more like some sort of indication that my spirit has a lot to say or express. And there isn't always enough \*time\* and \*space\* for all of it.

I once had an idea of developing an art \*style\* called "Work in Progress" where I'd do the art in such a way that some areas would be complete, some would be sort of half done, and others would still be at the sketch stage. The idea was to show the various stages of my artwork as a way to express how each moment is important to the full piece. It's based on my phrase \*I'm a work in progress, making progress\*.
