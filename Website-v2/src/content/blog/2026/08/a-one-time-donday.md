# Morning

Sometimes it amazes me how one day things can seem to be going well. And then the next day, things seem to be falling apart...LOL Sunday, I was on cloud 9. Yesterday, most of the day was a storm cloud. Today, I reflect on all of this.

I made a post on Bluesky where I "announced" that Monday would now be known as "Donday", since I was "done" with Monday...LOL I announced that pretty early in the day. And although the day got a little better after that, it was quite a "Monday" Monday...hehehe

Thankfully, my best friend reminded me I was in a club with her. The I-don't-mind-mondays club. I'm so grateful she called that out. I read that after the day was over. But it was quite impactful to my spirit as I laid my head to rest last night. I replied that I'd be back to the club next week...LOL

Cherie was the star of my day. In one case, quite literally...LOL 🤩 She was the sort of light that God uses to keep me focused on Christ instead of on the "storms" of the day. Things like our Rando Monday and movie Tuesday are a huge help on days like what I was experiencing yesterday. I'm so grateful for the blessings God has given us through our friendship.

The last part of my day was actually pretty good. I spent the last few hours finishing up a project from the weekend. I finally have Notesprout working on my Supernote devices! I'm using it today for my Bible study and work notes. I'm not using it to write this blog entry. There are still some things I need to work out to improve the experience on the Supernote devices before I'd want to do a blog entry. But I'm getting close.

Today is going much better so far. My spirit is much lighter. And the struggles of yesterday did not keep me down. I slept well and woke feeling pretty good ❤️
