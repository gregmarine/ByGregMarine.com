I'm having one of those seasons in my life when I have thoughts of wishing I were "normal." I don't actually wish that in reality. But it is a thought that crosses my mind when I do things that are uniquely me. I have grown to appreciate my uniqueness. It's just that I sometimes feel it difficult to navigate certain situations or circumstances that I feel so many others easily get through.

This is somewhat related to that thought...yesterday, I went all day without looking or being outside. It felt a bit weird. And I'm not really even sure why I did that. But it was also something that felt pretty good. I wasn't sick or anything. Light wasn't bothering me. I just didn't want to look out. I wanted to remain closed off from the neighborhood for some unknown reason. I almost posted about the experience. I decided not to because it seems some weird that I even did that.

I did post about something else that was a bit different. I drove around the entire Tampa Bay on Saturday. My sister asked to hangout. I was happy to do that. We didn't have any real plans. I had thoughts of checking out a new mall or something. New to us, that is. But then I felt like trying to drive around the entire bay instead. One of my shoes had fallen apart as soon as I got to her house. So, the first thing we did was go to a Sketchers outlet. After that, I asked her if she'd want to drive around the entire bay...LOL She wasn't sure what I meant at first. But I tried to describe it to her. She didn't mind because she just wanted to hangout with me. Whatever I wanted to do, she was willing to go along...hehehe Taking a drive seems normal. And it probably is. It's just that my motivation to take a drive seems odd to me.

This morning, I was almost in literal tears because my days haven't been normal at all lately. Things keep popping up out of nowhere. I wished that I could have a least one "normal" day. From my point of view, most people wouldn't have felt the way I was this morning. It's just that the littlest thing can throw me off for the rest of the day. I don't feel that's a normal thing. I think I manage this well, all things considered. It's just that it can be so exhausting. And boy am I tired of this right now...LOL

Yesterday, I wrote up [part 2](/blog/2026/08/our-may-vacation-2026-part-2/) of [Our May 2026 Vacation](/tags/may2026vacation/). I did that to help cope with this imbalance in my routine. That vacation, and all of my vacations with Cherie, have been a sort of anchor point for my mood. When I think about those vacations, especially the one in May, it get a sense of calm in my spirit. In fact, that's one of the reasons I chose the Queen Mary photo for my [Relax](/blog/2026/06/relax/) photo frame. I look at that multiple times each day when I'm in the bathroom. It has recentered me many times ever since I put it on the frame.

I don't have a kitchen table. I don't want a kitchen table. The last time I had my family over for dinner, I brought in my patio table and chairs for us to eat at. I think that was the last time I've used that table too...LOL This was like 3 or 4 years ago...LOL This is also the first time in my life when I haven't had a kitchen/dining table. I'm certainly not against them. I just don't want one right now. It seems a little strange. But here we are...LOL Mom wanted to buy me one when I first moved here. We even went table shopping. I picked one out and told her I'd wait a little bit before getting it to be sure I wanted that one. That was 4 years ago...LOL

I've tried to be normal most of my life. It never felt right and I was rarely myself. These days, I'm doing my best to be who I am without concerning myself with doing normal things just to try to fit in. I still don't feel as though I have a place in many of the circles I'm in. My actual circle is quite small. But I fall into those other circles like having a "family." By that, I mean my parents and siblings. I never quite had a place in my family. And I feel I don't belong. I don't think they feel that way, though. I don't think they understand the struggle that has been for me...LOL I'm okay with that now, though. It used to bother me. And I do wish I was more normal for them. But, yeah, whatever...HAHAHA

This week, I'm back on my pescatarian diet. I've been leaning towards it for quite a while now. So, I decided I would commit to it for at least this week. I'm not against eating meat. And I may have some from time to time. I'm not going to stick to a strict diet like I did a few years ago. This is more of a "feeling" I'm having lately. This is definitely not a normal diet. I know very few who don't eat meat.
