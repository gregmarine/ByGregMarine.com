I'm having one of those seasons in my life when I have thoughts of wishing I were "normal." I don't actually wish that in reality. But it is a thought that crosses my mind when I do things that are uniquely me. I have grown to appreciate my uniqueness. It's just that I sometimes feel it difficult to navigate certain situations or circumstances that I feel so many others easily get through.

This is somewhat related to that thought...yesterday, I went all day without looking or being outside. It felt a bit weird. And I'm not really even sure why I did that. But it was also something that felt pretty good. I wasn't sick or anything. Light wasn't bothering me. I just didn't want to look out. I wanted to remain closed off from the neighborhood for some unknown reason. I almost posted about the experience. I decided not to because it seems some weird that I even did that.

I did post about something else that was a bit different. I drove around the entire Tampa Bay on Saturday. My sister asked to hangout. I was happy to do that. We didn't have any real plans. I had thoughts of checking out a new mall or something. New to us, that is. But then I felt like trying to drive around the entire bay instead. One of my shoes had fallen apart as soon as I got to her house. So, the first thing we did was go to a Sketchers outlet. After that, I asked her if she'd want to drive around the entire bay...LOL She wasn't sure what I meant at first. But I tried to describe it to her. She didn't mind because she just wanted to hangout with me. Whatever I wanted to do, she was willing to go along...hehehe
