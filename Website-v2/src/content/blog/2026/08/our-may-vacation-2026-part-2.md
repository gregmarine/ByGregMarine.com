Welcome back to the next installment of [Our May 2026 Vacation](/tags/may2026vacation/)!!! So sorry it has taken so long to get back to this story. It was an amazing vacation. My lack of writing about it is almost shameful...LOL No, not really. But it does feel a little off to have not finished writing about it. Life be lifin', you know?! LOL

At the end of this entry, I'll include a link to the tiny bit of vlogging I did that day. I only recorded the first half of the day. Apparently I didn't do anything after a short clip when we got back to Disneyland Resort after our mid-day break...LOL

I started my day off with a bit of surprise. My phone's battery was down to the 30s! The night before, I had run out of space for photos and videos. So, I started the process of clearing data. When I went to bed, I did what I always had done...I plugged it in and turned on white noise. At that time, the battery was in the 80s I believe. When I woke up, I was concerned...LOL Not only was I thinking I wouldn't get any photos or videos that day, I wasn't sure I'd even been able to use it for my park tickets...LOL That's probably one of the reasons I didn't capture much that day. I was thinking the battery might die on me...LOL I reseated the charger and left it on charge until breakfast.

This was our first breakfast together on this vacation. We were so happy to be eating outside. This was the first time we'd had breakfast outside together. So, it was special for that reason. But it was also special because we take communion together at all our breakfasts.
