I tend to make tweaks to things in my life to see if I can make some improvements here and there. Sometimes those tweaks don't work out. Sometimes they become permanent or spin off into new and exciting things. Lately, I've been doing a lot of that and some of those things are truly making things seem better.

Today, I tried something new. I typically have a small e-ink photo on my keychain of my best friend, Cherie. She goes with me everywhere...hehehe The last few times I went to Disney, I decided to take the mini photo frame I have for photos she sends me. It's e-ink as well. With how much fun that was at Disney, I decided to bring that frame with me to breakfast today at First Watch. I've been going there every other Saturday and I had been setting her tiny photo next to my apple juice. Today, I had that mini 4 inch frame to put next to the apple juice. I like having her photo with me because it makes it feel like she's there with me.

That felt so good that I decided to try the same thing at dinner tonight. I don't normally go out for dinner. But I just felt like doing that today. I went to Olive Garden and took Cherie's mini photo frame along with me. She enjoyed a nice dinner with me as I read a few chapters of Wool...hehehe

This little tweak might be something I keep doing. I do a lot of things physically alone. But I often share those experiences with her through photos, videos, or just text descriptions of what I'm up to. She often does the same with me when she's out and about. Having her little photo there is a nice touch that makes my heart happy. The tiny photo on my keychain is a bit small for a dinner table...LOL The mini photo frame works much better.

My sleep schedule tweak a made a few weeks ago is working out great. I'm typically up until 1:30am and I wake up around 7:30am. I feel incredible each morning and I have so much more time to do things. I'm especially productive in the evening after dinner. Before this switch a few weeks ago, my evenings were very short and quite unproductive. It felt like it was almost bedtime shortly after dinner...LOL

On top of my sleep schedule tweak, I added a sleep mask to the mix a little over a week ago. Wow! That thing has done wonders for me! It completely blocks out light. I can have the lights on in my room and I can't see them at all. I didn't know such a sleep mask existed. It's incredible and has made my sleep patterns so much better.

Something else that's new is I'm rebuilding Notesprout and Paintsprout...LOL I've been super focused on rebuilding Notesprout with lessons learned from the version of it I've been using since I finally got it finished a few months ago. I got some ideas for this from the fact I didn't like how Biblesprout was coming along. And I had an idea for a prayer app as well. I decided those two were best as extensions of Notesprout. So, in my redesign work, I've made Notesprout capable of having extensions to enhance its feature-set. And it works in such a way that I can install the extensions at will on the devices I have Notesprout. So, if I don't need all of the features from all of the extension on certain devices, I don't have to install them. It makes for a much better app and a better user experience on whatever device I'm using it on at the time.
