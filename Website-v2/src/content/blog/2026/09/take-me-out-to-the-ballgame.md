🎼 Take me out to the ballgame! Take me out to the crowd! 🎶

Yesterday, I spent the day with my sister and her husband. We went to the Tampa Bay vs. Boston MLB game. After that, we went out for dinner. And ended our day with some ice cream. It was a fun filled day with family! And some friends of my brother-in-law's.

I sure did miss Cherie! Not only did we not get to do our weekly video call, but it was also my first time going to a baseball game in recent years without her. I kept wanting to hear her laugh at my silliness and my \*daaaaaaaayo\*...LOL But I didn't let that stop me from having so much fun. Something that helped was that I recorded a vlog of the baseball portion of the day! While I was enjoying my time at the game and with my sister and her husband, it felt like Cherie was there with me because I knew she'd get to share in the experience 🤗

I also love that I can go back and experience the day when I watch this vlog in the future. That's definitely a benefit of doing a vlog and this blog. I was very impressed with how well my new setup worked! I was able to capture some amazing footage with my new cameras. And the audio works so well. It feels like I'm there when I watch the video 🤩

The vlog covers most of the day. So, I give you, [A Day at the Ballgame](https://youtu.be/jhhsvCudAYk) 🤩
